Privacy Policy

Nineveh: Home Library. Effective October 16, 2026.

Nineveh is a catalog for the books you own. It is built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what that means.

We do not collect any data

Nineveh has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. We have no server that receives your catalog.

Your catalog stays on your device

Every book you add, along with your shelves, notes, formats, and read dates, is saved only in your browser's or app's storage on your device. You can delete any book in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything. Backups you export (CSV, Excel, or JSON) are files you control, saved wherever you choose.

Book lookups

When you look up a book, the app sends only the ISBN or the search text you typed to these services to get the book's details and cover:

  • Open Library (Internet Archive). See the Internet Archive terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Books. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Cover images are loaded from those services as well. If you import an Audible export, its covers load from Amazon (m.media-amazon.com). Like any website, they can see your device's IP address when your device contacts them. Nothing else about you or your library is sent.

Camera

If you choose to scan a barcode, the camera is used on your device only to read the ISBN. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Children

Nineveh is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
